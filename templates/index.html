Connect Smartphone Camera

Turn your mobile phone into an AI traffic camera using local Wi-Fi!

1
Install IP Camera App:

Android: Download IP Webcam (Play Store)
iPhone: Download DroidCam or iVCam (App Store)

2
Connect to Same Wi-Fi:

Ensure your laptop/PC and smartphone are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

3
Start Server in App & Enter Stream URL:

Open the app, tap Start Server, and copy the video URL displayed (e.g. http://192.168.1.15:8080/video).
